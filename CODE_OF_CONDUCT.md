# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Flutter Power Pack welcoming, respectful, and professional for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate constructively, assume good faith, give and receive actionable feedback, respect boundaries, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Contributions should be relevant to the project's purpose and should not expose private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized content, doxxing, spam, malicious code, credential disclosure, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/Alqudimi) or the contact email in the README. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as confidentially as practical. The maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, or close contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
